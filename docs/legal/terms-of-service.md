## 2. Words used in these terms

- **Service:** Jantrik School, the online software that Jantrik runs for institutes: the web app,
  its mobile screens, its SMS messages, and any later mobile app.
- **Module:** a part of the Service that is switched on per Institute, such as Attendance, Fees
  or Exams. The core (students, guardians, classes, staff) is always on.
- **Institute Data:** all information the Institute, its staff or its guardians put into the
  Service, or that the Service creates from it (for example attendance, fee and result records).
  Most of it is about **children**.
- **Staff Users:** people the Institute adds as owner, admin, teacher, accountant or staff.
- **Guardians:** parents or guardians whose mobile number the Institute records for a student.
  They can sign in with an SMS code to see their own children only.
- **Plan:** the prices, limits (such as the number of students and the daily SMS ceiling) and
  billing cycle in the Order Form.

## 3. Accounts and sign-in

3.1 **No public sign-up.** Jantrik creates the Institute's account and invites its first owner
by mobile number. The owner then invites the Institute's other staff. Guardians get access
because the Institute records their mobile number for a student.

3.2 **The Institute decides who has access.** The Institute chooses who is a Staff User, what
roles they have, which sections they teach, and which guardian numbers are on each student's
record. It removes access when someone leaves. Jantrik does not check whether a person really
works at the Institute or is really a child's guardian.

3.3 **One mobile number, one login.** Everyone who uses the same mobile number shares one
sign-in. Whoever holds that phone sees every child linked to that number. The Institute should
record each guardian's own number. [to verify: whether this needs a stronger warning in the
admission form.]

3.4 **Users keep their sign-in safe.** Staff Users must keep their phone and password to
themselves, sign out of shared computers, and tell the Institute at once if they think someone
else used their account. The Institute must make sure its Staff Users follow this.
