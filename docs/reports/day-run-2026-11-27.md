## Task 2: the test year from the test clock ([#37](https://github.com/omerFixesBugs/jantrik-school/pull/37))

- **Approach:** every test institute's academic year is the calendar year of the test clock: the
  harness's FakeClock date (Asia/Dhaka) when a test installs one, otherwise `todayIn`. Dates that
  only need to lie inside the year are built from it; dates that must be before or after today
  come from today and hold on 1 January and 31 December. The suites stay on the real clock (a
  fixed test day broke them, D6). Test-only: no product code, migration or `.env` change.
- **Proof by simulation** (D6): the db (571) and API (1011) suites, on the real clock and as if it
  were **1 January 2027 00:30**, **15 June 2027 10:00** and **31 December 2026 23:30** (Dhaka):
  all pass, except 2 tests per suite that fail only inside the simulation (Postgres timeouts do not
  fire under libfaketime; a lock file's age when only Node's clock moves) and pass on the real
  clock. Shared (738) and web (153) pass at 1 January.

  failed from 00:00 to 06:00 Dhaka time on every 1st; the trial test ended on 2026-12-31; the
  admission cycle closed in January; attendance reports cannot look back before the year's start.

## Task 5: P7b, reconciliation and the review queue ([#39](https://github.com/omerFixesBugs/jantrik-school/pull/39))

Built on #38 from the approved plan with P7b-A to P7b-O; every test was run and seen failing
before its code. SSLCommerz is asked about every payment at +5 min, +30 min, +2 h and +25 h, and every
success goes through P7a's one decision path (a query answer never posts by itself); +25 h expires
what never completed; a sweep, a nightly re-queue and heal on read survive a Redis loss. The review
queue (OWNER/ADMIN; the accountant sees it): **apply** the money to chosen dues (one receipt, dated
the day SSLCommerz took it), **refunded outside**, or **dismiss** only what holds no money (money is
never dismissed); every decision needs a note, is audited and is made once. The online payments
report, the closing's online line, the dashboard tile. The database enforces that only a person's
decision moves a payment out of review. The automated commit review flagged two issues in its
first migration (two dismissals at once; the payment-to-intent match from one side only), both
fixed test-first.

- **Review round 1: one must-fix**, reproduced: a void after a partial apply (৳800 applied, ৳400
  refunded outside) forgot the refund, so the full ৳1,200 could be applied again (records for
  ৳1,600 of ৳1,200). Now a void keeps the refund and apply is capped at the money minus it. Two
  should-fix (one payment refunded outside twice through a race; a live receipt detached from its
  intent by an injection) and hardening (the decider is the signed person; a review's amount is
  its event's; BDT only; an undocumented SSLCommerz status is "unknown", never "no success"; the
  report counts duplicate and unmatched money). All fixed with failing tests first.
- **P7a2's final fixes merged in**; the payment-to-intent match function, which both PRs
  redefine, keeps the stricter union of both.
- **Review round 2 (final): no must-fix**; it confirmed the money invariants (applied + refunded
  outside = the money once decided; money never dismissed; one live payment per intent). Three
  should-fix, fixed with failing tests first: a payment validated at ৳0.00 could never be decided
  (now an unknown amount, which can be refunded outside); after a Redis loss a paid payment could
  miss its +25 h check (now re-listed for 72 h); an item with no money could never be dismissed
  when SSLCommerz's answers contradict each other. Plus: a retried apply after a void answers 409;
  an intent can no longer take money already refunded outside as an item (and `settle` sends it
  to review); the report counts each payment once; apply losing to the counter answers 409
  DUE_COLLECTED.
- Playwright first failed in CI for the same sign-in code limit as P7a2; the spec now signs in
  once per person and uses its own child. Gates: db 632, API 1207 tests, web build.
