## Task 2: the test year from the test clock ([#37](https://github.com/omerFixesBugs/jantrik-school/pull/37))

- **Approach:** every test institute's academic year is the calendar year of the test clock: the
  harness's FakeClock date (Asia/Dhaka) when a test installs one, otherwise `todayIn`. Dates that
  only need to lie inside the year are built from it; dates that must be before or after today
  come from today and hold on 1 January and 31 December. The suites stay on the real clock (a
  fixed test day broke them, D6). Test-only: no product code, migration or `.env` change.
- **Proof by simulation** (D6): the db (571) and API (1011) suites, on the real clock and as if it
  were **1 January 2027 00:30**, **15 June 2027 10:00** and **31 December 2026 23:30** (Dhaka):
  all pass, except 2 tests per suite that fail only inside the simulation (Postgres timeouts do not
  fire under libfaketime; a lock file's age when only Node's clock moves) and pass on the real
  clock. Shared (738) and web (153) pass at 1 January.
- **Found on the way:** the platform billing and institutes tests used the UTC month, so they
  failed from 00:00 to 06:00 Dhaka time on every 1st; the trial test ended on 2026-12-31; the
  admission cycle closed in January; attendance reports cannot look back before the year's start.
  No product bug: every failure was a test assumption.
- **Left for you (TY-4):** the unique test phones are drawn from a counter whose floor grows one a
  second from 1 November 2026, so the range runs out around **21 May 2028**; then every test that
  makes a user stops with F-6's message. Changing that trades away the guarantee that a lost
  counter file never repeats a number, so it is in the table below.
